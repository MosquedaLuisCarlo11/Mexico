Mexico’s Atmosphere:
Climate, Weather, and Ecosystems

Explore the intense weather patterns, diverse climates, and unique biological biomes shaped by Mexico's global position.

Atmospheric Forces

Mexico's geographical location spans the Tropic of Cancer, subjecting it to extreme atmospheric forces. The northern half is dominated by high-pressure subtropical zones, creating arid deserts, while the southern half is influenced by trade winds and monsoon moisture, nurturing dense rainforests. This intersection of circulation cells creates a land of high biodiversity, but also exposes it to severe weather events.
